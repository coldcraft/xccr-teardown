How the live site works

  • 227664 + Enter calls t.php, which resets a 6000-s server timer. Needs a same-origin Referer; no 10-min lockout.
  • Any other number goes to l.php?N; always 2 bytes back. 126 archived guesses, zero hits.
  • Counter rises 300 sat/h while the timer is alive, decays when it isn't, floors at the doorB wallet balance.
  • Clearing a slot rewrote keyB1 a to D and removed the D line (0 / 7 = D1a, 7 DOGE).
  • Only other file: b1-x.png, a quarter of a QR code, online since Apr 2022.
