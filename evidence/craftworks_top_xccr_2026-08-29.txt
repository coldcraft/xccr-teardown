                The History (2004-2008)
                    The site went unnoticed for a couple of years, until the user Stormalong on the
                        Unfiction forums created a post describing the discovery:
                        Typing 227664 as mentioned in the Slashdot post resulted in a grid appearing,
                            and in the subsequent days the website changed to also show the question: ARE YOU
                                HIM?
                    Upon seeing this, Stormalong's curiosity grew. What did this mean? He began to dig further,
                        investigating the website's files.

                    The next GIF, i3.gif, was solved by moving the columns up until the tops of the blue
                        bars matched. The message extracted was:
                    HIM MUST BE ANSWERED WITH CLUE FOUND IN I2 GIF END
                    Following that hint, contestants looked “past skin deep” in the frames of i2.gif.
                        The GIF had 7 frames with 6 columns. By taking column selections from various frames (as
                        indicated by 227664), the message found was:
                    HE IS THE CODE ON SLASHDOT
                    Participants then searched Slashdot for users matching those clues. They found a user
                        227664. Another clue came from the first letters of each line of
                        readme.txt:
                    t f e y e t
                    This corresponded to another valid user on Slashdot: tfeyet, which led to more
                        profile clues and further puzzle branching.
